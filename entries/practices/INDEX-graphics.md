# practices: graphics and geometry

- [An auto-correction whose target is measured on skin has a skin tone baked into it](auto-correction-keyed-on-skin-encodes-a-skin-tone.md) — Ordinary complexions span 0.33-0.78 luma, so a fixed face-luma target lightens dark faces and darkens pale ones; meter light on the frame and keep only variation on skin. `image-processing`, `camera`, `accessibility`
- [A 3D detail placed at a 2D-looking offset ends up inside the mesh and vanishes silently](3d-features-sink-into-the-surface.md) — There is no draw order to lean on; the part is added, lit and drawn, just behind an opaque surface, so it reads as a missing mesh. `3d`, `graphics`, `webgl`
- [A node mirrored with a negative scale also mirrors every rotation applied beneath it](mirrored-scale-inverts-rotation.md) — One value driving both halves of a symmetric rig moves them apart; the rig looks right at rest and breaks on the first animated frame. `3d`, `animation`, `scene-graph`
- [In a perspective view, a world-space proximity test rejects the target the player is aiming at](perspective-aim-needs-a-cone.md) — Aim is a ray, so a sphere around the cursor only works at the cursor's depth; test angular distance, and resolve to one best-aimed candidate. `3d`, `game-design`, `input`
- [A raised-cosine contour is flat at its own rim, so contours sum into a height field with no creases](raised-cosine-contours-compose-without-seams.md) — Zero value and zero slope at the rim means primitives add with no crease and no impulse, which is what lets a body stay glued to the surface. `graphics`, `terrain`, `simulation`
- [Scattering points with `i * stride % size` lays them on a lattice, not at random](modular-stride-scatter-is-a-lattice.md) — Consecutive samples step by a constant, so "random" speckle rules itself into diagonals as density rises; use an R2 low-discrepancy sequence. `graphics`, `procedural-generation`, `randomness`
- [A wrongly wound triangle is not drawn inside out, it is not drawn at all](backface-culling-deletes-geometry.md) — Back-face culling discards it silently, so a missing highlight reads as a state bug rather than a geometry one; assert winding where meshes are built. `graphics`, `webgl`, `3d`
- [Brightening a lamp cannot widen the pool of light on a wall of coplanar faces](lamp-power-cannot-widen-the-pool.md) — N·L fixes the lit radius by geometry, so more power only clips the centre; move the light off the surface and wrap the diffuse term. `graphics`, `lighting`, `webgl`
- [A metal surface lit only by punctual lights is black everywhere except its highlight](metal-needs-an-environment.md) — Metals have no diffuse term, so adding or brightening lamps cannot fill them; a prefiltered environment is the missing term. `graphics`, `rendering`, `pbr`
- [A colour palette is calibrated to its shading model, not to the screen](palette-is-calibrated-to-its-shading-model.md) — Flat fills are final values; as albedo they get multiplied down by ambient, N·L and fog, and the dark end vanishes first. `graphics`, `color`, `rendering`
- [A renderer's colour management ends at its materials, so a hand-written shader writes linear values to an sRGB buffer](shader-output-needs-srgb-encode.md) — The whole gradient renders several stops too dark, which reads as a badly picked palette or a bad light rig rather than a missing encode. `graphics`, `webgl`, `color`
- [Two shapes sharing an edge must trace one curve, and only a self-symmetric one reverses by a sign flip](shared-edge-needs-a-self-symmetric-curve.md) — Asymmetry breaks the reverse traversal, leaving a hairline gap that looks like a placement bug. `geometry`, `graphics`, `procedural-generation`
- [Framing a box in a perspective camera is a per-corner solve, not a bounding sphere](framing-a-box-is-a-per-corner-solve.md) — A sphere fit is orientation-blind and empties a portrait frame; projecting extents instead clips the near corners. `3d`, `camera`, `layout`
- [A camera solved to fill the frame converges on a face-on view and throws the perspective away](auto-framing-collapses-to-face-on.md) — Projected area peaks looking down a planar subject's normal, so the fill objective is what flattens the picture; clamp the angle range. `3d`, `camera`, `game-design`
- [A collision world's segment list is not a display representation, and drawing it one primitive per segment shows the joins](segment-list-needs-rechaining-to-draw.md) — Rounded ends poke through their neighbours and a curve renders ribbed; chain the segments into polylines first. `graphics`, `game-design`, `geometry`
- [Comparing normals to an axis mis-reports winding; the signed volume of a closed mesh does not](signed-volume-settles-winding.md) — Off-axis parts are correctly oriented while pointing inward, so the heuristic buries the one real inversion in 70% false positives. `3d`, `graphics`, `geometry`
- [In a width-scaled projection, camera height must scale with the inverse aspect ratio](camera-height-scales-with-inverse-aspect.md) — A narrow viewport's nearest visible ground collapses, so one huge near polygon fills a third of the screen and reads as a culling bug. `3d`, `camera`, `layout`
- [A level terrace cut into a slope always has a steep uphill bank, and no blend shape avoids it](terrace-bank-is-set-by-the-face.md) — The cut has to be given back within the blend, so the bank is about 1.4x the face's own slope whatever the window shape. `terrain`, `level-design`, `game-design`
- [Blending toward the nearest point on a path creases wherever the nearest point flips](nearest-point-blend-creases-at-the-medial-axis.md) — At a hairpin the two legs hold different values, leaving a step down the middle of the corridor; average over every sample in range instead. `graphics`, `terrain`, `procedural-generation`
- [A path across a height field must be solved from the surface's own slope, not given a height of its own](path-on-a-surface-must-be-solved-not-drawn.md) — Two independent functions agree only by accident; heading `G/g` into the fall line gives grade `G` by construction. `procedural-generation`, `terrain`, `geometry`
- [A shading rule keyed on a grid-sampled slope renders narrow features as a row of teeth](grid-sampled-derivative-beats-against-a-shading-threshold.md) — Smooth geometry and smooth normals with teeth in the shaded render means the fault is in the colour; low-pass the slope before any threshold. `graphics`, `terrain`, `debugging`
- [A painter's sort ranks a polygon by one depth, so a long surface is overpainted by the small things lying beside it](painter-sort-ranks-a-whole-polygon.md) — The runway is transformed, clipped, projected and queued correctly, and is simply not there; cut it into strips and bias coplanar layers. `graphics`, `3d`, `canvas`
- [A level-of-detail ring selected by cell centre must overlap its neighbour by more than one cell](lod-rings-must-overlap-by-a-cell.md) — The gap is half a coarse cell wide, and the background showing through it reads as water or cloud rather than as missing geometry. `graphics`, `terrain`, `level-of-detail`
- [The XZ plane seen from above is left-handed, so a yaw written as (cos, sin) is the mirror of a scene-graph rotation](xz-plane-yaw-is-mirrored.md) — Both conventions are one angle called yaw, they agree at 0 and pi, and off-axis the mesh is drawn ninety degrees from the collider of the same object. `3d`, `graphics`, `geometry`
- [The camera pitch that fits a ground plane to a frame is asin(footprintAspect / canvasAspect)](ground-plane-pitch-from-canvas-aspect.md) — Pitch picked by eye is wrong at every other window size, and sizing a 3D canvas to the subject's 2D footprint wastes the frame; clamp so it never solves to straight down. `3d`, `camera`, `layout`
- [In a 3D scene, assert that every interactive cell round-trips from its own pixel back to itself](pick-round-trip-proves-the-3d-contract.md) — Clicking and looking passes near top-down and fails once the camera is orbited; keep the projection maths out of the GPU so the sweep runs headlessly. `3d`, `testing`, `input`
- [A radial kernel sized in pixels is an ellipse in grid terms whenever the cells are not square](radial-kernel-on-a-non-square-grid.md) — No single pixel sigma is round in cells, so the blob starves along one axis and the low count reads as a bad threshold. `graphics`, `canvas`, `layout`
- [The textbook local image warp folds near its own rim, and worst at small displacements](warp-falloff-must-be-flat-at-the-rim.md) — Gustafsson's falloff is non-monotonic below ~0.55r and creases each handle; one flat at the rim holds to 2r/3. `graphics`, `geometry`, `image-processing`
- [A filter radius fixed in pixels is calibrated to one subject size, not to the image](filter-radius-follows-subject-scale.md) — The same blur erases a distant face and barely touches a near one, which reads as a badly tuned slider; derive it from a measured feature. `graphics`, `image-processing`, `computer-vision`
